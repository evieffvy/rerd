Features

Everything your team needs,
nothing it doesn't

Built for teams who value clarity, reliability, and not having to babysit infrastructure.

Zero-config deployments

Push to Git and Forge handles the rest. Automatic builds, container packaging, and rollouts — no YAML archaeology required.

Real-time observability

Logs, metrics, and traces in one unified view. Drill into any request in milliseconds — no switching between six different tools.

Security by default

Secrets management, network policies, and SOC 2 compliance baked in. Ship with confidence, not crossed fingers.

Intelligent autoscaling

Traffic spikes handled automatically. Scale from 0 to millions of requests without a 3am incident page or a hefty bill.

Team collaboration

Role-based access, audit logs, and per-environment permissions. Your ops team will stop sending angry Slack messages.

Any stack, any cloud

Node, Python, Go, Rust, or anything else. Deploy to AWS, GCP, or your own infra. One CLI, infinite flexibility.

How it works

From code to production
in three steps

No consultants. No week-long migrations. No excuses.

1
Connect your repository

Link GitHub, GitLab, or Bitbucket in under 30 seconds. Forge auto-detects your stack and configures a sensible build pipeline.

2
Push your code

Every push triggers a build, runs your test suite, and deploys — automatically. Preview deployments for every pull request, included.

3
Ship and sleep soundly

Forge monitors your deployment, rolls back on anomalies, and pages only when it matters. You focus on features, not firefighting.
